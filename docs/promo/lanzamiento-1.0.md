## Reddit · r/PathOfExile2 (inglés, etiqueta «Tool»)

**Título**

```text
New to PoE2 with 1.0? I made a free site that checks your character: what you skipped and what to fix first
```

**Texto**

```text
Hi! I posted an early version of this a few weeks ago and got useful feedback. It's now updated for 1.0, so here it is for everyone starting their first campaign.

https://exilecopilot.com/?lang=en&ref=reddit

What it does:

- Short act guides (acts 1-4): the route, the permanent rewards you shouldn't skip, and how the act boss works. There's a 4-line summary on top. Acts 3 and 4 are still marked as drafts.

- "Did I skip anything?": paste your gear with Ctrl+C, add a few numbers from your character sheet, and it works out which permanent rewards you're missing.

- Quick character review: it asks for a few things you can see in game and tells you the 3 things holding you back the most, in order, with how to fix each one.

- Your passive tree: paste a Path of Building 2 code and it shows the best notables near you. The numbers come from the real PoB2 engine, running in your browser.

- Small helpers: which support gem fits a skill, and which of two items is better for your character.

How it's different from existing tools:

- Maxroll and Mobalytics give you a build to follow. This is for people who don't have a build yet: it looks at YOUR character and tells you the next thing to fix.

- Path of Building is the best tool there is, but it's a lot for a first campaign. Here PoB2 runs behind a simple question.

- poe2db and the wiki have all the data. The guides link to them as sources.

- It's fully available in Spanish, with the Spanish client's names. That's why I started it.

How I made it (rule 11):

- I'm one player, not a studio. I design it and test it while playing. The code was written with a lot of help from AI coding assistants.

- The advice comes from fixed rules and game data (poe2db, the official passive tree export, PoB2). In the mentor chat, an AI model (Groq) only words the answer: it can't recommend something the rules didn't produce. When it doesn't know, it says so.

- The act guides were checked by hand against the wiki, poe2db and Maxroll. The sources are linked in each guide.

Things to know:

- Free, no sign-up, no ads. Not affiliated with GGG.

- It's made for the campaign. It won't help much with endgame.

Feedback is very welcome, especially from new players: what confused you, what's wrong, what's missing. There's a "Report a problem" link at the bottom of the site.
```
